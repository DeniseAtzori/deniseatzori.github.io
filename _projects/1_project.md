**IAnahid** is a Heritage Image Annotator: an application for visualizing large collections of heritage images, automatically clustering them, and correcting/refining their automatic indexing.

Try it out on Hugging Face Spaces: [huggingface.co/spaces/ENC-PSL/IAnahid](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ENC-PSL/IAnahid)
