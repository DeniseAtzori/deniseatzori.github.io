I am a PhD student in Computational Literature at the [Laboratoire LATTICE](https://www.lattice.cnrs.fr/) (CNRS / ENS-PSL / Sorbonne Nouvelle), based at the École nationale des chartes (ENC-PSL). My research sits at the crossroads of computer vision, natural language processing, and the humanities, with a focus on synthetic data generation and few-shot / in-context learning strategies for the recognition and annotation of historical documents and cultural heritage collections — from ancient Chinese character recognition to the automatic classification of photographic archives in art history.

I am also involved in the development of [IAnahid](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ENC-PSL/IAnahid), an application for the visualization, automatic clustering, and correction of automatic indexing of heritage image collections.
